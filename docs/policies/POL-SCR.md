## Purpose

Orrinwick knows which third parties touch federal customer data and manages the risk they carry.

## Scope

All Orrinwick staff and contractors, and every information resource marked in scope in mas/mas-record.json.

This policy covers these information resources from the scope record:

| ID | Resource |
|---|---|
| T1 | Mobile push notification service |
| T2 | Transactional email delivery service |
| T3 | Telephony provider (SMS and voice) |
| T4 | Application performance and error tracking service |
| T5 | Cloud infrastructure provider |
| N6 | Third-party risk management process |

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | KSIs |
|---|---|---|
| SCR-1 | Each third party in the data path is listed in the scope record as a third-party resource with its function, the data it receives and its authorisation status. | KSI-SCR-MIT |
| SCR-2 | A third party is reviewed before first use and each year after. Notification providers (T1 to T3) receive no alert summaries. | KSI-SCR-MIT |
| SCR-3 | The GRC Lead monitors third-party advisories, outages and ownership changes every month and records any that affect the offering. | KSI-SCR-MON |
| SCR-4 | Open-source and third-party software components are tracked in a software bill of materials for each release. | KSI-SCR-MIT, KSI-SCR-MON |

## Indicators and validation

| KSI | Name | Automated methods | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|
| KSI-SCR-MIT | Mitigating Supply Chain Risk | `mit-iac-supply-chain-policy`, `mit-live-supply-chain-risks` | pass |
| KSI-SCR-MON | Monitoring Supply Chain Risk | `mon-iac-upstream-monitoring`, `mon-live-upstream-advisories` | pass |

Status is a simulated validator result. Class C needs at least two automated methods per KSI (`FRC-CSX-VVK`).

## Exceptions

An exception needs a written request, a named risk owner, a compensating control and an expiry date of no more than 90 days. The GRC Lead records approved exceptions in docs/security-decision-record.md. An expired exception is a policy violation.

## Enforcement

Violations are reported to the GRC Lead. Staff violations follow the personnel security process (N5).
