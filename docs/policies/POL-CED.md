## Purpose

Staff know the security duties of their role and the company checks that the training works.

## Requirements

| ID | Requirement | KSIs |
|---|---|---|
| CED-1 | All staff complete general security training within 30 days of hire and once every 12 months. | KSI-CED-RAT |
| CED-2 | Staff with production access, support staff with tenant access and incident responders complete role-specific training before access is granted. | KSI-CED-RAT |
| CED-3 | Engineering staff complete secure software delivery training each year. | KSI-CED-RAT |
| CED-4 | Staff on the incident and recovery roster take part in at least one recovery exercise each year. | KSI-CED-RAT |
| CED-5 | The GRC Lead reviews training completion and effectiveness each quarter. Effectiveness evidence includes phishing exercise results and incident root causes. | KSI-CED-RAT |

## Exceptions

An exception needs a written request, a named risk owner, a compensating control and an expiry date of no more than 90 days. The GRC Lead records approved exceptions in docs/security-decision-record.md. An expired exception is a policy violation.

## Enforcement

Violations are reported to the GRC Lead. Staff violations follow the personnel security process (N5).
